Pricing and common questions

Prices depend on the job. Message me what you need and I will quote you before I start.

How do I get a quote?

Send the details on WhatsApp or email. I reply with a price and a timeline.

What do I receive?

Documents come as Word, Excel or PDF files, ready to print or send. Websites are hosted on Netlify or Vercel and connected to your own domain.

What should I bring for KRA help?

Your ID, the phone number and email linked to your account, and any KRA documents you already have. I will tell you if anything else is needed.

What should I bring for government service help?

Your ID and the phone number and email you want linked to your account. For a business or company, details of the owners or directors. For a marriage application, details of both partners. For a passport, your ID and any old passport; for a child, the birth certificate and both parents' IDs, and you attend the biometrics appointment yourself. For CRB services, your ID number and your phone. For NTSA services, your ID and, for a vehicle, its registration number. I will tell you if anything else is needed. Official fees are shown on eCitizen, the portal or the credit bureau, and I quote my own service fee before I start.

Can you type up my handwritten notes?

Yes. I turn handwritten notes into clean Word documents and tell you if I change a name or figure.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. Client details stay confidential.

Where do you work?

I am based in Nakuru and work with clients across Kenya. Most documents and web work can be done online.
